## Verification Instructions

Since the official House and Senate directory pages block automated access, you'll need to manually verify these domains:

### Step 1: Verify House Members
1. Visit: https://www.house.gov/representatives
2. Use your browser's search function (Ctrl+F / Cmd+F) to search for each domain
3. Check that each domain appears in the official listing

### Step 2: Verify Senate Members
1. Visit: https://www.senate.gov/senators/index.htm
2. Use your browser's search function to search for each senator's name
3. Click through to verify their official website matches the domain in this list

### Step 3: Check for Retired/New Members
- Verify that no domains belong to members who have left Congress
- Verify that all current members are included

### Step 4: Special Verification Needed
- Confirm **norton.house.gov** is appropriate (D.C. delegate)
- Confirm leadership domains (democraticleader, republicanleader) are appropriate
- Check for any recently elected members who may not be in the gem yet
